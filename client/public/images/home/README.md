# Home page images: provenance

Real photos from ORTAM AI classes. Faces, logos, documents and personal
details are blurred or cropped out because the people in them did not
consent to publication:
- graduation-handshake.webp: certificate handshake, cropped below faces
- class-applause.webp: class applause, documents blurred
- cyber-ai-session.webp: Cyber AI session, faces and papers blurred
- cyber-lab.webp: cyber lab session, faces, screen logo and browser bar blurred

AI-generated (Figma generate_image, gemini-3.1-flash-image). Atmosphere only,
never presented as real students or graduates:
- ai-private-learner.webp: woman learning on a laptop at home in Tel Aviv, face turned away
- ai-org-workshop.webp: corporate AI workshop around a long table, instructor at a wall screen
- ai-hero-projector.webp (+ -sm): classroom in warm daylight with a ceiling projector and a blank projection screen, students seen from behind (hero; the presentation and beam are drawn in code). Retouched: the screen's lower frame was moved up to just below the projected area, keeping the students' heads in front
- handshake-mask.png: AI-generated flat silhouette of two people shaking hands, used only as a shape for the particle drawing
- course-claude-code.webp, course-ai-studio.webp, course-ai-agents.webp, course-cyber-ai.webp: AI-generated (Figma, gemini-3.1-flash-image) atmosphere images for the four featured course cards; no faces, no readable text
