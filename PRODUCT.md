# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Two audiences of equal weight for the training offer:
- Organizations and companies that train their employees in AI (today the larger share of the business).
- Private individuals looking to learn AI for career change or advancement (the audience the site should grow).

Secondary: employers looking to hire AI-trained graduates (placement).

## Product Purpose
ORTAM AI is an AI training center in Tel Aviv. Its main service is AI training programs and courses, including a dedicated Cyber AI track. Secondary service: placement of AI-trained graduates. Marginal service: an accelerator for entrepreneurs and small businesses, currently low volume and not promoted.

Success on the site: organizations book a consultation and fit call; private individuals ask for more information over WhatsApp.

## Positioning
Hands-on AI and Cyber AI training delivered in real classrooms, for both corporate teams and individuals, with a placement path for graduates.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Bilingual site, Hebrew (RTL, primary) and English.
- Organizations action: "תאמו שיחת ייעוץ והתאמה" to the contact form.
- Private action: "קבלו מידע בוואטסאפ" to WhatsApp 972523381822.
- Accelerator appears only as a footer link.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: ORTAM AI. Logo colors: navy and orange.
- Home headline (approved): "לומדים AI. עובדים עם AI."

## Evidence on Hand
- 15 programs, about 15 classes of about 20 students, about 300 graduates.
- Clients section titled "בין לקוחותינו": ועד עובדים כלל ביטוח, צה"ל, Tokomni, Omnitelecom, אוניברסיטת אריאל, לומדים ומתקדמים, עמותת בוגרי ממר"ם, ההסתדרות, בנק לאומי, CyberGo. More logos to be added later.
- Real classroom photos exist, but there is no consent from the people in them: faces, logos, documents and personal details must be blurred or cropped out.
- No measured satisfaction percentage, no testimonials yet. Do not invent either.
- AI-generated imagery may be used for atmosphere, never presented as real students or graduates.

## Product Principles
- Training first: every page leads with the courses.
- Two doors of equal weight, organizations and individuals.
- Only true claims and numbers.
- Real classrooms over stock imagery, with privacy protected.
